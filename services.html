What we do

Seven service lines, one crew: we plan, build, power and maintain the sites our clients depend on.

Telecom site build, operate & maintain

End-to-end delivery of telecom sites — from civil build to day-to-day operation and upkeep.

Grid power development

Design and delivery of grid power solutions that keep critical sites reliably energised.

ICT consultancy

Independent, technically-grounded advice to help clients plan and de-risk their ICT investments.

Network quality & field services

On-ground audits, optimisation and field support that keep network performance where it should be.

General supplies & merchandise

Sourcing and supply of equipment and materials that keep projects moving on schedule.

Renewable energy deployment

Property & infrastructure build

Structural and infrastructure builds delivered to engineering standard, start to finish.
