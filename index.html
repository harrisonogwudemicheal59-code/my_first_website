Our background

Estadysta Nigeria Limited was founded to close the gap between telecom operators' ambitions and the reality on the ground. Our people are engineers and technicians who have spent over fifteen years working on live networks — and they bring that field-tested judgement to every job.

Today we serve local and international clients across telecoms, power and infrastructure, backed by OEM certifications and professional accreditations. From independent tower audits for the Nigerian Communications Commission to fibre route surveys for Nokia and building works for the Nigerian Army, we deliver to specification, on schedule and safely.

Quality, quantity and compliance — on every project, without exception.
